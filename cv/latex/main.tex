\section*{Academic profile}
Physician and RENACYT Level III researcher with graduate training in Clinical Epidemiology and Biostatistics at Universidad Científica del Sur. Experience includes public health and clinical research, research methodology, statistical analysis, critical appraisal, scientific writing, evidence synthesis, university teaching, thesis advising, peer review, and scientific editing. Research interests include public health, clinical epidemiology, women's and maternal/perinatal health, violence, mental health, and other priority health problems, with experience in observational, population-based, secondary-data, bibliometric, and evidence-synthesis research.
